# AMA-51 Capital Flow and Liquidity Reserve

`CapitalFlow` retains currency, due and effective times, recognition state,
evidence, priority, expiry, and supersession history. Recognized payables are
already reflected in NAV and are never reserved again. Only active,
not-yet-recognized withdrawals, contractual outflows, and minimum liquidity
reserve reduce risk capital. Planned deposits are retained for disclosure but
do not increase risk capital before recognition. Unknown recognition and a
reserve exceeding NAV fail closed.
