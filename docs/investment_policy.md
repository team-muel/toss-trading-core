# Investment policy

- Version: `investment-v0-draft`
- Status: `DRAFT / NOT APPROVED`
- Objective: verify the complete investment operating path, not maximize return.
- Universe: versioned allow-list of US-listed broad-market and cash-like ETFs.
- Direction: long-only; no margin, shorting, options, leveraged ETFs, OTC assets,
  foreign-currency borrowing, or unsupported order types.
- Base investment currency: USD. KRW reporting is separate and uses an explicit FX
  observation and timestamp.
- Decision cadence: daily. Rebalancing is weekly or monthly unless policy says
  otherwise.
- Liquidity horizon, mandatory reserve, benchmark, and user loss capacity require
  explicit approval before Paper promotion. Until then target generation blocks.
